# Easy Authenticator — Privacy Notice (publication draft)

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Publisher: MasoomDev.

Easy Authenticator generates one-time authentication codes. Account secrets and related metadata may be stored on your device using operating-system-protected storage. The application also contains optional cloud sign-in and backup functionality; when enabled, data may be transmitted to the configured cloud provider. Do not describe the current version as strictly offline-only.

Before publishing, verify exactly what data is sent, what encryption is applied before transmission, where cloud data is stored, its retention/deletion process, and which subprocessors are involved. Disclose crash reporting, analytics, and telemetry if any are enabled. Provide an accurate contact email and rights-request process.

**Publication blocker:** This is a draft, not a verified privacy notice. Review the actual application implementation and publish a final URL before distributing publicly.
