EASY AUTHENTICATOR - DRAFT PRIVACY NOTICE
Last updated: 27 October 2026
Publisher: MasoomDev

Easy Authenticator generates time-based authentication codes. Authenticator secrets may be stored locally using operating-system-backed secure storage. Optional sign-in and cloud backup functionality exists in the application source and may process account and backup information through Supabase when configured and used.

Before public release, verify and disclose: exactly what account information and authenticator secrets are transmitted; whether backups are encrypted before upload; encryption key handling; service region and retention; deletion process; subprocessors; diagnostic data collection; user rights; and a verified support/privacy contact.

Do not describe this application as exclusively offline or as collecting no data without validating all enabled functionality.

Keep your recovery codes separately. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove backups or cloud-stored records.
